 KafkaFlow

 What is KafkaFlow?

KafkaFlow is an open-source, .NET-based framework built on top of Confluent’s Kafka client. It offers a structured, maintainable way to build Kafka producers and consumers 
in .NET applications, with a middleware-centric architecture that simplifies authorization, serialization, error handling, and more
