    A Kafka log is an append-only, ordered sequence of events. Each event has a timestamp and a description.

    Kafka Connect can bring data from (existing database or a SaaS product) into Kafka topics, making it available for new services to consume


    Producer: service or connector publishes an event to a Kafka topic

    Consumer: background service It listens (subscribes) to the Kafka topic for new events.
    after receiving and processing the event, enriches the data if needed and then writes the final processed record to MongoDB.
    New relic errors logs
    1) Problem: Kafka consumer loses connection to the broker.
    Solution: found the error in logs and network team adders the issue.
    2) Problem: MongoDB records are incomplete after processing.
    Solution: Review and fix the data enrichment logic in the consumer application.

    Sample event flow of a loan processing system:
    [Loan Application Service]
    └── produces → topic: loan.application.submitted

    [Credit Check Service]
    └── consumes loan.application.submitted
    └── produces → topic: loan.credit.checked

    [Underwriting Service]
    └── consumes loan.credit.checked
    └── produces → topic: loan.underwriting.completed

    [Loan Approval Service]
    └── consumes loan.underwriting.completed
    └── produces → topic: loan.approval.finalized

    [Disbursement Service]
    └── consumes loan.approval.finalized
