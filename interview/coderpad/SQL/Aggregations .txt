Aggregations (COUNT, SUM, GROUP BY).

Write a query to find how many employees work 
in each department. 
Show department name and the employee count.


select d.name , count(*) as '# of employees'  from departments d
inner join employees e on d.id = e.department_id
group by d.name 

Write a query to find the total salary paid per department. 
Show the department name and the total salary.

select d.name, sum(e.salary) as total_salary from departments d 
inner join employees e on d.id = e.department_id
group by d.name
