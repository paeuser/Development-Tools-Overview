Common Insurance Industry Terms

This table includes key terms used within the insurance industry, from policies to claims.

Term Explanation
Policy A legal contract between an insurance company and the policyholder, defining the terms of coverage.
Premium The amount of money the policyholder pays to the insurance company for coverage.
Deductible The amount the policyholder must pay out of pocket before the insurance company pays for a covered loss.
Claim A formal request to an insurance company for payment under the terms of the insurance policy.
Underwriting The process of evaluating the risk of insuring a person or asset and determining the premium and coverage.
Policyholder The individual or entity who owns the insurance policy.
Coverage The amount of risk or liability covered for an individual or entity under the terms of the insurance policy.
Exclusion A specific provision in a policy that states what the insurer will not cover.
Actuary A business professional who deals with the measurement and management of risk and uncertainty.
Rider An optional add-on to an insurance policy that provides additional coverage or benefits for an extra cost.
Liability Insurance Insurance that protects the policyholder against claims of a third party for injury or property damage.
