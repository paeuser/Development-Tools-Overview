Common Financial Terms

This table includes key financial terms that may be useful for your interview preparation at BHG Financial.

Term Explanation
Asset Anything of value owned by an individual or company that is expected to provide a future benefit.
Liability A financial debt or obligation that arises during business operations.
Equity The value of a company or asset after all liabilities have been paid off.
Credit Score A numerical rating used by lenders to assess a person's creditworthiness and likelihood of repaying debt.
Secured Loan A loan that is backed by collateral, such as a house or car.
Unsecured Loan A loan not backed by collateral. Lending is based on the borrower's creditworthiness.
Principal The original amount of money borrowed, separate from any interest or fees.
Interest The cost of borrowing money, usually expressed as a percentage of the principal.
Default The failure to repay a debt according to the loan agreement terms.
Underwriting The process of evaluating a loan applicant's creditworthiness and risk.
Loan Origination The end-to-end process of a borrower applying for a loan and the lender processing it.
Recourse Loan A loan that allows the lender to seize both the collateral and other personal assets if the borrower defaults.
Creditworthiness A lender's assessment of how likely an individual or business is to repay debts.
Collateral An asset (e.g., property, equipment) pledged by a borrower to a lender to secure a loan.
Yield The income returned on an investment. For a loan, it's the total return a lender earns.
APV (Annual Percentage Yield) The real rate of return earned on an investment, taking into account the effect of compounding interest.
SBA 7(a) Loan A small business loan guaranteed by the Small Business Administration (SBA), a common product for a lender like BHG.
